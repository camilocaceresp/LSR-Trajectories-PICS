Why this review

A gap in the evidence

Survival from critical illness has improved markedly, yet complete functional recovery remains rare. PICS affects the majority of adults who survived critical illness across at least one domain — yet its longitudinal course remains poorly characterised.

01

No multi-domain synthesis exists

Prior reviews examined single domains in isolation or focused on point-prevalence rather than trajectories of change over time.

02

Natural history is unknown

Without pooled quantitative estimates of recovery trajectories, it is impossible to identify critical windows for intervention or build predictive models.

03

Bayesian priors are needed

This review will generate domain-specific tau-squared estimates to parameterise informative priors for future Bayesian analyses of PICS.

Outcome framework

Five PICS domains

Outcomes are pooled per domain and subdomain at each pre-specified time point, from ICU discharge to beyond 12 months.
